## Design Context

### Users
DAT5565 instructor and group members reviewing the final project results.

### Brand Personality
Clear, restrained, evidence-based. Chinese and English appear together.

### Aesthetic Direction
Light background, simple layout, readable charts and tables for course presentations.

### Design Principles
- Read final result files directly; distinguish measurements from interpretations.
- Keep sample size, protocols, units, and limitations visible.
- Use consistent model colors and chart scales.
- Support offline viewing, narrow screens, and printing.
